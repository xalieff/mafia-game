Mafia

A party game of secret roles and quiet suspicion. Everyone plays from their own phone, and nobody needs an account.

How to play
  • Needs 4 to 15 players. Everyone gets a secret role.
  • Mafia pick someone to eliminate each night. Doctor protects one person. Detective checks whether someone is Mafia.
  • Each morning, the town learns who died, talks it through, then votes someone out.
  • Town wins when all the Mafia are gone. Mafia wins when they equal or outnumber the Town.
